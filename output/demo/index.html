A clear first step

Start with one piece.

Capture a garment or select up to eight images. The app resizes photos, queues recognition and takes you into review.

Camera preview supports capture and retake, with a device-camera input and file upload as alternatives. Desktop startup recovery was verified; physical camera capture still needs device testing.

A human stays in control

Review, then it’s yours.

Local Qwen3-VL suggests structured garment details. Untouched forms prefill automatically, while your edits stay intact. Save and move to the next piece.

This screenshot came from a real sample-file upload and live recognition. The test photo was kept out of training feedback. Recognition suggestions are editable and may be wrong.

One request, three possibilities

Your closet, recombined.

Enter a brief, optionally choose a starting piece, and create looks. Retrieval finds relevant pieces; the planner builds complete owned outfits; the selected provider chooses plans; validation checks the result.

The screenshot shows Quick lexical RAG, not a language-model response. OpenAI or local AI provides generated styling advice. Failure falls back to explicitly labeled Quick matches.
